- Booking success page adds Open My Studio.
- Booking magic/SMS links normalize to https://silkcrayon.com instead of the old Vercel preview hostname.
- Booking SMS starts with Silkcrayon Studios and uses clearer reminder wording.
- Buy Studio Hours and Gift Studio Time now have purchase-specific post-payment confirmation screens.
