# Silkcrayon Studio OS V20.4.8 — Relaunch offer + hour packs

Includes all V20.4.7 changes.

## 2 Hours for £100
- New public `/relaunch-offer` page.
- £100 instead of £120.
- Adds 2 hours of studio credit to My Studio; no booking date required at purchase.
- One paid relaunch offer per customer/email.
- Available through 31 August 2026.
- Linked from the homepage and Choose Your Session.
- Existing My Studio 2-hour offer changed from £110 to £100.
- Post-session offer email now points to the relaunch offer and says £100.

## Studio hour packs
`/buy-hours` is now a permanent prepaid pack product with an interactive 3–10 hour slider/wheel:

- 3h £170 (save £10)
- 4h £220 (save £20)
- 5h £270 (save £30)
- 6h £320 (save £40)
- 7h £370 (save £50)
- 8h £420 (save £60)
- 9h £470 (save £70)
- 10h £520 (save £80)

Hours go into the customer's existing studio-credit balance and can be split across future sessions.

## Gifts
Gift Studio Time remains 1–8 hours at the standard £60/hour so gifts are not silently discounted or changed by the pack pricing.

## Stripe / accounting
`studio_payments.amount_pence` stores the actual paid amount.
`list_amount_pence` stores normal £60/hour value.
`discount_amount_pence` stores the saving.
The relaunch offer uses discount code `RELAUNCH_2H_100`.

No new SQL is required specifically for V20.4.8.
