# V20.2.6.2 — Final pre-migration hotfix

## Cancellation requests
Cancellation requests are now actionable directly from Studio Overview:
- Review request
- Cancel + card refund
- Cancel + studio credit
- Cancel with no refund/credit
- Decline request and keep session
- Open full booking

No money/credit outcome is automatic. Owner explicitly chooses and confirms it.
Every resolution is written to booking_events and the customer receives an outcome email.
Card refunds use Stripe idempotency. Credit restoration is protected by a database unique index.

Run:
`supabase/v20-2-6-2-cancellation-hotfix.sql`

## Analytics
Growth & Analytics now includes:
- selectable 7 / 30 / 90 day periods
- page views by day
- unique visitors by day
- hourly visit distribution in Europe/London
- existing top pages and acquisition metrics use the chosen period

No page-view tracking change was required; this visualises the data already being recorded.
