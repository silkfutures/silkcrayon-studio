# Silkcrayon Studio OS V20.4.9

Includes all V20.4.8 changes.

## Relaunch offer post-purchase choice
After purchasing 2 Hours for £100, the success screen now asks:

1. **Book my 2-hour session now**
   - Goes straight to the customer's banked-hours booking calendar.
   - Preselects a 2-hour Vocal Recording session.
   - Customer chooses the available date and time.
   - No second Stripe checkout: the session uses the two banked hours.

2. **Choose my date later**
   - Goes to My Studio.
   - The two hours remain in the customer's credit balance until used.

The logged-in My Studio £100 offer now returns to this same choice screen after successful Stripe payment.

## Existing banked hours
The standard My Studio banked-hours booking flow remains available. It now also supports an optional `?hours=` preselection so purchase journeys can hand off directly to the correct duration.

## SQL
No new SQL is required for V20.4.9.
