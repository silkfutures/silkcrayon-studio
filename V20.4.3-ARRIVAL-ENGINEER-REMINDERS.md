# V20.4.3 — Arrival experience + engineer reminders

Includes all V20.4.2 changes.

## Reminder timing
Vercel cron currently runs `15 9 * * *` and the automation selects bookings whose booking_date is tomorrow.
That means the reminder is sent the calendar day before at roughly 10:15 UK local time during BST and 09:15 during GMT.

## Customer communication
- Confirmation email now includes a visual Getting to Silkcrayon section with the lane entrance and a link to `/getting-here`.
- If an engineer is already assigned at confirmation time, their profile name/photo/phone are included.
- Day-before reminder email loads the assigned engineer dynamically from `staff_profiles` and includes name, photo and phone.
- Day-before SMS includes the assigned engineer name/phone and tells the customer to text them when they reach the lane.
- My Studio includes a permanent Getting Here link.

## Public Getting Here page
- Explains Portland House has sides on Bute Street and West Bute Street.
- Explains the lane runs through the middle and the studio entrance is in the lane.
- Uses the supplied lane/Bute Street/West Bute Street reference images.
- Parking can be paid at machines or through MiPermit.
- Parking is stated as free after 6pm on both streets, per the studio-provided information.
- Daytime rates are deliberately not hard-coded; customers are told to check the machine/MiPermit.

## Staff backend
- Adds `photo_url` to staff_profiles.
- Admin Staff page can store a profile photo URL or upload JPG/PNG/WebP directly to a public Supabase Storage bucket.
- Reminder/confirmation emails always read engineer name/photo/phone from the assigned staff profile.
- Nathan seed: customer-facing engineer name `Nathan`, phone `07577724944`, photo `/images/staff/nathan.webp`.
