# V20.2.6 — Release Candidate

## Customer booking
- Recording sessions now support 1–7 hours. Eight hours remains the separate £450 Full Day product.
- Mobile booking no longer wastes width on a permanent left step gutter.
- Duration/date/time rows are full-width horizontal touch rails.
- Browser zoom is available again.
- 30p system-test is absent from public UI and rejected server-side unless explicitly enabled in preview.
- Past booking times are rejected server-side.

## Marketing incentive
- Homepage email signup offers a one-time 5% reward on the next eligible recording booking.
- The booking email opt-in carries the same incentive.
- Reward applies automatically by email address and is only consumed after a successful discounted payment.

## SMS
- A supplied phone number can receive essential, non-promotional booking confirmations/reminders.
- Promotional email and SMS remain separate opt-ins.
- SMS logs correctly record channel=sms.
