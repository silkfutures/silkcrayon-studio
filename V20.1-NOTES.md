# V20.1 — Contact database + Marketing Centre

The supplied Wix export contained 165 rows and is deduplicated into 162 CRM contacts:
- 13 subscribed
- 148 never subscribed
- 1 unknown

Run `supabase/v20-1-contacts-marketing.sql` after the V20 leads migration.

New:
- `/admin/contacts` searchable legacy + current contact database
- `/admin/marketing` opening-offer campaign composer
- one-button sync of eligible subscribers to a `Silkcrayon Marketing` Resend Segment
- Resend Broadcast draft/send workflow
- campaign history
- future bookings and enquiries keep CRM current

Safety:
- `never_subscribed`, `unknown`, and `unsubscribed` contacts are not automatically sent marketing
- repeat bookings no longer accidentally remove a previous marketing opt-in
- website enquiries enter CRM without being silently subscribed

Optional Vercel variable:
`MARKETING_EMAIL_FROM=Silkcrayon Studios <info@silkcrayon.com>`
